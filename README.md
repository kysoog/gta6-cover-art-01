Afterhours turns local photos into original multi-panel sunset-noir cover art, with editable titles, three colour grades and full-resolution PNG export.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
Open http://localhost:8080 in Chrome.

Drop one or more photos onto the uploader or load the included procedural sample set. Select panels A, B or C, click a thumbnail to assign its photo, drag to pan and scroll to zoom. Choose a square, portrait or wide post, then export a PNG at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. Everything runs locally; no photos are uploaded.
